Assignment

Context
PlayerNation's mission is to help every football (soccer) player understand and improve their performance.

One of the core experiences we're building is a Ratings Engine that converts raw match data into meaningful insights for players and coaches.

Think about products such as FIFA player ratings, chess ratings, Whoop recovery metrics, or any system that transforms large amounts of data into simple and understandable scores

A good rating should:

- Reflect performance accurately
- Be easy for users to understand
- Be explainable
- Reward impactful actions
- Be fair across age groups, playing styles, etc
- Improve as more data becomes available

Please consider whether multiple dimensions of performance may better represent a player's or coach's strengths and weaknesses, and not just one rating.

There is no single correct answer. We are interested in how you think about the problem, the tradeoffs you make, and how you design a system that could evolve over time.



Instructions
You will be provided with event-level football match data. Dataset:
https://github.com/koenvo/wyscout-soccer-match-event-dataset

Please use the FIFA World Cup dataset..

Using this data, design and build a ratings system that generates ratings for:

- Every player
   - Every coach You should decide:

- What ratings should represent
- Whether a single score, multiple attributes, or a combination of both best represents performance
- How ratings are calculated
- What metrics matter
- How different actions should be valued
- How ratings evolve over time
- How ratings should be presented to users
- Any assumptions required to make the system work

You are encouraged to think beyond traditional statistics and consider how a player's or coach's profile can be represented in a way that is engaging, actionable, and easy to understand.



Deliverables
1. Methodology Document

A document explaining:

- Your approach
- Key assumptions
- Rating logic
- Important tradeoffs
- How you would evolve the system over time
   - Any limitations of your current approach Keep this focused on decision-making.
2. Working Solution

A working solution that processes the provided match data and generates ratings for every player and coach.

The implementation can be:

- Script
- Web application
- Notebook
- CLI tool
- Any format of your choice

Please include clear instructions for us to run, access, and evaluate the solution.



What We Will Evaluate
- Product thinking
- Ability to work with ambiguous problems
- Quality of assumptions
- Data and analytical thinking
- Simplicity and explainability of the solution
- Ability to balance short-term practicality with long-term vision
- Quality of execution



This is intentionally an open-ended problem. We are more interested in your reasoning than in a perfect answer.
